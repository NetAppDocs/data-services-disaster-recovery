= Quick start for NetApp Disaster Recovery
:hardbreaks:
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/get-started/

[.lead]
Learn how to configure NetApp Disaster Recovery, ensuring your system meets the requirements to successfully discover workloads and configure replication plans to prepare for failover.

.image:https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NetAppDocs/common/main/media/number-1.png[One] Review prerequisites 

These tasks require the _Super admin_ or _Console admin role_.

[role="quick-margin-para"]
* link:https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-setup-admin/concept-agents.html#agent-installation[Ensure you've installed a Console agent^]
* link:https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/console-setup-admin/reference-iam-disaster-rec-roles.html[Review Disaster Recovery user roles and assign permissions to users who need them^]
* link:../get-started/dr-prerequisites.html[Ensure your system meets these requirements]
* Ensure you meet the appropriate requirements for your workload type:
** link:../get-started/reference-vmware-prerequisites.html[VMware prerequisites]
** link:../get-started/task-kubernetes-requirements.html[Kubernetes prerequisites]
