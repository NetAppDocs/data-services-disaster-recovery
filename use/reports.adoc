= Create reports in NetApp Disaster Recovery 
:hardbreaks:
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/use/

[.lead]
NetApp Disaster Recovery reports can help you analyze your disaster recovery preparedness. Reports provide insights into the replication plans including the number of resource groups, VMs or Kubernetes clusters, and datastores covered by the plan. They also display compliance status, a summary of jobs, and backup settings including recovery point objective (RPO) and retention count. 

You can download reports in PDF, HTML, or JSON format. 

The report download link is valid for six hours after you generate it.  

.Steps 

. Log in to the link:https://console.netapp.com/[NetApp Console^].
. From the NetApp Console left navigation, select *Protection* > *Disaster recovery*.
. From the NetApp Console left navigation bar, select *Replication plans*. 
. Select *Create report*.
. Choose which *Workload* to scope the report to: Kubernetes, VMware, or all. 
. Choose the *File type* (PDF, HTML, or JSON) and *Time period* (between the past one and seven days) that the report should cover.
+
image::report.png[Screenshot of create report]
. Select *Create* to generate the report. 
+
NOTE: The report can take a few minutes to generate. 
. On the Replication plans dashboard, select *Download report*.
