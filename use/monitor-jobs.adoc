= Monitor NetApp Disaster Recovery jobs
:hardbreaks:
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/use/

[.lead]
Monitor NetApp Disaster Recovery jobs to view their history, status, and details. Tracking job status and progress enables you to catch failed or stalled operations before they affect recovery readiness.

== View jobs 

include::../_include/iam-app-admin-viewer.adoc[]

.Steps 

. Log in to the https://console.netapp.com/[NetApp Console^].

. From the NetApp Console left navigation, select *Protection* > *Disaster recovery*. 
. From the menu, select *Job monitoring*. 

. Review jobs, including their timestamps and status.
. To view details for a job, select its row. To download job details for offline review, select the action menu and then select **Download job details**. 
+
[TIP]
When you review job details, Disaster Recovery shows the user who initiated the job. If the responsible party is listed as "system," Disaster Recovery initiated the job.  
. To refresh information, select *Refresh*. 


== Cancel a job

You can cancel a job that's in progress or in a queued state if it's stuck or to prioritize a pending operation.

include::../_include/iam-failover-app-admin.adoc[]

.Steps 

. From the NetApp Console left navigation bar, select *Protection* > *Disaster recovery*. 
. From the menu, select *Job monitoring*. 


. On the Job monitoring page, note the ID of the job that you want to cancel. 
+
The job must be in an "In progress" or "Queued" state. 
. Select the action menu then **Cancel job**.
