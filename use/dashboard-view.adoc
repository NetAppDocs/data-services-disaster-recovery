= View the health of your disaster recovery plans in NetApp Disaster Recovery
:hardbreaks:
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/use/

[.lead]
Using the NetApp Disaster Recovery Dashboard, you can determine the health of your disaster recovery sites and replication plans. You can easily assess which sites and plans are healthy, disconnected, or degraded. You can also review recent warnings and failed jobs, see which workloads are protected and are not, and see an overview of capacity. 

include::../_include/iam-app-admin-viewer.adoc[]

.Steps 

. Log in to the https://console.netapp.com/[NetApp Console^].

. From the NetApp Console left navigation, select *Protection* > *Disaster recovery*. 

. From the NetApp Disaster Recovery menu, select *Dashboard*. 
+
image:dr-dashboard.png[NetApp Disaster Recovery Dashboard]

. Review the health information in the dashboard: 

* *Sites*: View the health of your sites. A site can have one of the following statuses: 

** *Running*: The site is connected, healthy, and running. 
** *Down*: The site is not reachable or having connectivity issues. 
** *Issue*: The site is having connectivity issues. 
+ 
To see details, select **View all sites**. 

* *Replication plans*: View the health of your plans. A plan can have one of the following statuses: 

** *Ready*
** *Failed*
** *Warning*
+ 
To review replication plan details, select *View all replication plans*. 

. Review *Recent warnings and jobs* to see the most recently executed jobs and warnings and their associated statuses. Select *View all jobs* to see all jobs. 
. Review the *Discovery and protection status* to see the number of discovered workloads and a numeric breakdown of protected versus unprotected workloads separated by type. You can also review the total discovered capacity of all workloads and workloads by type.
