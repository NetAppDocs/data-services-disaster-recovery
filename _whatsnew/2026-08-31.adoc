=== Improved SnapMirror snapshot management

You can now trigger failover using the latest snapshot, which is the preferred method when you're not taking a new snapshot.

When selecting a specific snapshot baseline, SnapMirror transfer snapshots are hidden by default. This is because they can be deleted mid-failover, leading to an inability to reprotect the workload after failover due to "no common snapshot found" error. You can still use these snapshots, but will have to navigate an additional confirmation prompt to acknowledge the risk. 

For more information, see link:https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/data-services-disaster-recovery/use/failover.html[View the health of your NetApp Disaster Recovery plans on the Dashboard^].
