=== Improved dashboard
The Disaster Recovery dashboard has been refreshed with an enhanced experience that provides a comprehensive, at-a-glance view of your entire Disaster Recovery estate. The new dashboard includes:

* *Unified workload visibility* — Monitor both VMware and Kubernetes workloads alongside underlying infrastructure health from a single pane of glass.
* *Proactive issue surfacing* — The dashboard now automatically highlights environmental issues, enabling faster diagnostics and reduced time to resolution.
* *Capacity utilization* — Capacity metrics are now surfaced directly within the dashboard, eliminating the need to navigate elsewhere to understand resource consumption.

For more information, see link:https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/data-services-disaster-recovery/use/dashboard-view.html[View the health of your NetApp Disaster Recovery plans on the Dashboard^].

=== Improved reports

Reporting capabilities have been expanded to provide deeper insight into Disaster Recovery activity across your environment:

* Generate reports scoped to VMware workloads, Kubernetes workloads, or both — giving you the flexibility to tailor reports to your audience.
* Each report delivers a complete activity summary for any specified time period, ensuring full visibility into what occurred across your DR environment.

For more information, see link:https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/data-services-disaster-recovery/use/reports.html[Create NetApp Disaster Recovery reports^].

=== User job logging

Accountability and auditability have been improved with the introduction of user-level job logging:

* Every job executed within the service now records the user who initiated it, providing a clear audit trail for compliance and troubleshooting purposes.
* Jobs triggered automatically by the service itself are clearly identified with the designation of "system," making it easy to distinguish between user-initiated and service-initiated activity.

For more information, see link:https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/data-services-disaster-recovery/use/monitor-jobs.html[Monitor NetApp Disaster Recovery jobs^].

=== Performance and resource optimization

Various under-the-hood improvements have been made to reduce overall system resource consumption and increase performance across Disaster Recovery operations.
